Ksheera · Milked at four. At your door by six.
The demo brand: a dairy farm near Kanchipuram delivering A2 milk, curd, paneer and ghee across Chennai before sunrise. Ksheera means milk in Sanskrit. Name, phone and email can be changed in one file.

A working, clickable prototype of the milk delivery website with three sides: customers (shop, subscribe, track), the admin (dashboard and analytics) and delivery partners (route and deliveries). Sample data is preloaded. AI is not connected: every AI feature runs on sample rules in code and is labelled demo on screen, so the panel sees exactly how it will work.

4. Questions to ask the assistant

Order in plain words

  • Add 2 litres of A2 milk and a paneer
  • Send half litre buffalo milk and 1 kg curd tomorrow
  • 1.5 litres of toned milk every day

Delivery and orders

  • When will my milk arrive?
  • Do you deliver to Velachery?
  • Track my order
  • What's the last time to order for tomorrow?

Products

  • What is A2 milk?
  • How much is a litre of milk?
  • How long does ghee last?
  • Is buffalo milk good for kids?
  • What can I make with paneer?
  • Is your milk adulterated?

Subscriptions, payment, support

  • How do I pause my subscription?
  • Which payment methods do you accept?
  • Do I need to return the glass bottles?
  • My milk was sour hands over to a person

Anything else gets a polite “demo mode” reply. All sample questions are also one tap away on the /project page.

Say to “Say your order”

  • Two litres of A2 milk and one paneer
  • Half litre buffalo milk, 1 kg curd and a ghee for Sunday
  • Four buttermilk and a mango lassi in the evening

Ask the admin dashboard

  • What was revenue last week?
  • How much milk do we need tomorrow?
  • What are customers complaining about?
  • Which batches failed quality checks?

5. A five-minute demo

  1. Open the link and scroll the home story from night to sunrise.
  2. Tap Say your order, choose “Rendu litre paal and oru thayir”, then Add and check out → Continue as demo customer → Pay.
  3. On the confirmation, show the tracking steps, the arrival time and the Invoice.
  4. Open Ask Ksheera: ask “How long does ghee last?”, then “My milk was sour”.
  5. Go to /admin → Continue as demo admin → ask “Which product sells the most?” → open AI insights (forecast, sentiment, quality) → Orders to approve a held payment.
  6. Go to /delivery → Continue as demo delivery partner → show the route map and mark a stop delivered.
